AfriMart Sales Analytics Dashboard
A one-page sales dashboard for AfriMart covering revenue, cost, profit, margin and units sold across African markets, 2024 to 2026.

What's on the page?
1. Year filter: switch between 2024, 2025 and 2026.
2. KPI cards: revenue, cost, profit, margin and units.
3. Revenue by country: top 10 countries, led by Nigeria (1.62B) and Tanzania (1.25B).
4. Profit by product: Rice (50kg Bag) and Palm Oil (5L) lead with 955.84M and 604.69M.
5. Units sold by product: seven products, each between about 130K and 156K units.
6. Profit by country: Nigeria leads with 302.61M.
7. Monthly revenue trend: revenue and profit by month, January to December.

Data fields:
Country, Product, Units Sold, Revenue, Cost, Profit, Date.
Profit = Revenue − Cost
Margin = Profit ÷ Revenue

Number format:
Large values are shortened to K, M, B and T (for example 21.48M).

Prepared by: Emediong Bassey

Questions the dashboard answers:
Overall performance:
1. How much revenue, cost and profit did AfriMart generate?
2. What is the overall profit margin?
3. How many units were sold?
4. How does performance differ between 2024, 2025 and 2026?

Markets
5. Which countries bring in the most revenue?
6. Which countries are the most profitable?
7. Which markets are largest by revenue but weaker on profit? (Compare the two country charts.)

Products
8. Which products earn the most profit?
9. Which products sell the most units?
10. Do the best-selling products also make the most profit? (Detergent sells the most units but is near the bottom for profit.)

Trends and seasonality
11. How do revenue and profit move month by month?
12. Which months are strongest or weakest? (Revenue peaks around August.)
13. Is the gap between revenue and profit stable over the year?
