AfriMart Sales Analytics Dashboard
A one-page sales dashboard for AfriMart covering revenue, cost, profit, margin and units sold across African markets, 2024 to 2026.

What's on the page?
- Year filter: switch between 2024, 2025 and 2026.
- KPI cards: revenue, cost, profit, margin and units.
- Revenue by country: top 10 countries, led by Nigeria (1.62B) and Tanzania (1.25B).
- Profit by product: Rice (50kg Bag) and Palm Oil (5L) lead with 955.84M and 604.69M.
- Units sold by product: seven products, each between about 130K and 156K units.
- Profit by country: Nigeria leads with 302.61M.
- Monthly revenue trend: revenue and profit by month, January to December.

Data fields:
Country, Product, Units Sold, Revenue, Cost, Profit, Date.
Profit = Revenue − Cost
Margin = Profit ÷ Revenue

Number format:
Large values are shortened to K, M, B and T (for example 21.48M).

Prepared by: Emediong Bassey

Questions the dashboard answers:
Overall performance:
- How much revenue, cost and profit did AfriMart generate?
- What is the overall profit margin?
- How many units were sold?
- How does performance differ between 2024, 2025 and 2026?

Markets
- Which countries bring in the most revenue?
- Which countries are the most profitable?
- Which markets are largest by revenue but weaker on profit? (Compare the two country charts.)

Products
- Which products earn the most profit?
- Which products sell the most units?
- Do the best-selling products also make the most profit? (Detergent sells the most units but is near the bottom for profit.)

Trends and seasonality
- How do revenue and profit move month by month?
- Which months are strongest or weakest? (Revenue peaks around August.)
- Is the gap between revenue and profit stable over the year?
